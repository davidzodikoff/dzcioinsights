Reverbium Privacy Policy

Reverbium uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center may collect your username, scores, and achievements in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store payment information.

We do not collect, store, or share any additional personal information beyond what Apple's services require to function.

Reverbium uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized ads only, meaning your activity is not used to personalize advertising based on your behavior across other apps or websites.

AdMob may collect limited technical information about your device (such as device type, operating system version, general location derived from IP address, and a vendor-scoped device identifier) to deliver ads and prevent fraud. This data is processed by Google according to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Reverbium does not request permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. The "Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) does not affect Reverbium's advertising, as Reverbium does not engage in such tracking.

To learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use their services, visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

This app is not directed at children under 13.
