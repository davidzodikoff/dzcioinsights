Support

Reverbium is a fun and if you want competitive burp application. The Home page shows four archways to enter each one leading to an inner room of the Ancient Abbey. It also invites you to Join the Order which will give you two subscription options to remove adds and gain access to your lifetime statistics and detailed analytics into your most recent utterance.

The Inner Sanctum is where you can practice your vocal resonances (burps) and get them scored. Just hit the big glowing button that says "Unleash the Reverbium" and summon a burp. When you are done hit End Resonance, if it detects a burp it will score the burp and under all of the metrics will be an Again button to try again. You are scored on five different metrics, Power, Hold, Rumble, Depth and Texture.

The Journey is where your statistics are for your resonances. Until you are initiated into the Order you can see your Total Resonances, Average Total Score and what you need to progress to the next level. If you Join the Order it will remove ads and give you Lifetime statistics, Deep analytics on your most recent utterance and The Exalted Trio, your best three burps of all time.

Trials allows you to link your burp to the app's Gamecenter leaderboard and invite friends to compete.

The Scrolls are a more detailed history of the Ancient Order of the Reverbium.
